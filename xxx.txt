EPISODE 16: "EXPERTISE"

56. INT. WYATT’S KITCHEN — DAY
Wyatt is no longer sitting. He is pacing back and forth across
the small kitchen.
WYATT
If it were that simple, why haven’t you done it
before?
We’re talking about going to jail, or even dying
if things go south!!!
Wyatt stops pacing. He turns around and looks at Dixie,
desperate.
WYATT (CONT'D)
What do you know that I don’t?
Dixie doesn't blink. She just stares back at him, completely
unbothered by his outburst.

57. INT. CONVENIENCE STORE - NIGHT (FLASHBACK - HALLOWEEN 2015 /
DIXIE IS 15)
The store is decorated with cheap plastic pumpkins and eerie
orange neon lights.
DIXIE (15) staring directly into the camera, wearing a creepy,
plastic cat mask.
Through the eyeholes of her mask, Dixie glances across the
aisle. DESTINY (15), also wearing a cat mask, nods back. They
are waiting for the signal.
A YOUNG CLERK (20s) with an oversized mullet hairstyle is
completely distracted, deeply buried in a car magazine.
SUDDENLY— The store door flies open. A MAN and A WOMAN wearing
terrifying monster masks burst inside. One of them pulls out a
handgun and points it straight at the clerk's face.
MONSTER MASK ROBBER
(Screaming, creating the distraction)
Don't fucking move! Hands where I can see them!
The moment the gun is drawn, Dixie and Destiny move instantly.
No hesitation.
While the robbers keep the clerk frozen at gunpoint, Dixie and
Destiny slide behind the counter. With synchronized,
lightning-fast movements, Dixie digs into the cash register,
grabbing the money, while Destiny bags it. It is an organized
operation. It takes seconds.

58. EXT. GAS STATION - PARKING LOT — NIGHT (CONTINUOUS)
Dixie is sprinting for her life through the dark parking lot,
holding the money.
In the distance, the sudden, sharp wail of approaching police
sirens cuts through the night. Blue and red lights begin to
strobe against the walls.
The two monster-masked robbers split up, running in the opposite
direction.
Destiny is running right behind Dixie, but her foot catches the
edge of a curb. She slams hard onto the wet asphalt.
Before she can get up, the heavy shadow of a POLICE OFFICER
steps into frame over her. Before she can get up, a police
officer steps into frame over her and handcuffs her.
Destiny thrashes against the ground, looking up into the dark.
DESTINY
(Screaming, pure terror)
DIXIE! HELP ME! DIXIE!
Dixie stops running for a fraction of a second. She looks back
over her shoulder.
Dixie looks at her captured friend, then looks down at the money
in her hands, turns her head back around and sprints into the
pitch-black darkness, leaving her best friend behind.

59. INT. WYATT’S KITCHEN — DAY
SMASH CUT back to the present.
Dixie in the quiet kitchen. She slowly takes a sip from her
beer.
DIXIE
(Deadpan, terrifyingly calm)
You could say I have some experience.
SMASH CUT TO BLACK.
